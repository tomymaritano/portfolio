---
title: "Three.js + Sanity CMS"
line: 3D scenes that stay editable.
---

Three.js is powerful. Sanity is flexible. Getting them to play together took some figuring out.

## The challenge

I wanted a 3D surface whose content was editable in a CMS. Three.js scenes are imperative. Sanity data is async. React re-renders break 3D contexts. Performance was the first thing to die.

## What worked

Keep Three.js in its own component, isolated from CMS updates. Fetch all Sanity data before mounting the canvas — no async inside the scene. Use `@react-three/fiber` carefully (`useFrame` plus stale closures will haunt you). Memoize anything that touches the renderer.

3D and a CMS can coexist. The elegant architecture is often more complex than the one that stays smooth.
