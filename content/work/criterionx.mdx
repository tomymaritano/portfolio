## Problem

Business-critical decisions hide in `if/else` across a codebase. You cannot test them cleanly, and you cannot explain why a result happened when compliance asks.

## What I built

Criterion encodes decisions as pure, testable functions with schemas and an audit trail. Same input, same output. Every result includes *why*. Packages for React, Express, tRPC, and a CLI.

## One hard decision

Keep it a micro engine. Not a workflow product, not a model, not a marketplace. Small core, strict boundaries.

Docs at [tomymaritano.github.io/criterionx](https://tomymaritano.github.io/criterionx/).
