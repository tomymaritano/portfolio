## Problem

Business-critical decisions hide in `if/else` across a codebase. You cannot test them. When someone asks why, you cannot show a reason.

## What I built

Decisions as pure functions. Schemas. An audit trail. Same input, same output. Every result includes *why*. Packages for React, Express, tRPC, and a CLI.

## One hard decision

Keep it a micro engine. Not a workflow product. Not a model. Not a marketplace. Small core, strict boundaries.

Docs at [tomymaritano.github.io/criterionx](https://tomymaritano.github.io/criterionx/).
