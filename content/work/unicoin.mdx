## Problem

Crypto is hard to hold if the only story is speculation. Someone has to understand the backing before they buy.

## What I built

Product and UX. Journeys, compliance conversations, what a first buy should look like.

## One hard decision

Design for the traditional investor first. If they bounce, the rest of the book does not matter.
