## Problem

Note apps hold writing hostage: proprietary formats, an internet connection, a startup that might disappear. I wanted notes as plain files on my machine.

## What I built

Readied is an offline-first Markdown editor. CodeMirror 6, local storage, open source. What you type is what gets saved. No hidden transforms. Optional plugins. macOS first.

## One hard decision

Local-first is the product, not a feature. Sync can exist later. The file on disk has to stay readable if the app goes away.

Live at [readied.app](https://readied.app).
