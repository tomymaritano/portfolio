## Problem

Clinical software often splits the job: one surface to capture, another to write, another to send. The last mile — a document a clinician will actually sign — stays in Word and email.

## What I built

Psynth is the company I operate in. The work is the full path: identity, reports, generation, export. I treat it as a product system, not a pile of tools. This page does not describe patients, transcripts, or documents.

## One hard decision

Reuse ideas (section pipelines, extract, export contracts) as copies inside a surface, never as a leaky shared fork across products. The constraint is privacy and ownership, not cleverness.

The interesting call is always the same: what the clinician sends is a short, house-voice document — not a dump of the model.
