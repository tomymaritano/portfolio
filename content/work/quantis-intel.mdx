## Problem

A desk still assembles the report by hand — a slide, a spreadsheet, then something someone will send. The model can write. That is not the job.

## What I built

Financial reporting with AI. Messy inputs become a document a desk will send. Not a dump of the model.

## One hard decision

The report is the product. Generation that cannot land in a file a person will send does not ship.
