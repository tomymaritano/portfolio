## Problem

Dutch households juggle subscriptions. Expats have it worse: contracts in Dutch, unfamiliar providers, cancellations that take a week.

## What I built

Interviews with expats. An MVP. A mobile surface that turns a subscription list into something you can act on.

## One hard decision

Cut the catalog until a newcomer could cancel one bill without a local friend. Discovery over features.
