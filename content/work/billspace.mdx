## Problem

Dutch households juggle a pile of subscriptions. Expats have it worse — contracts in Dutch, unfamiliar providers, and cancellations that take a week.

## What I built

Product work across the lifecycle: interviews with expats, MVP scope, and the mobile surface that makes a subscription list something you can act on.

## One hard decision

Cut the catalog until a newcomer could cancel one bill without a local friend. Discovery over features.
