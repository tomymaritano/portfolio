## Problem

Course platforms sit on card processors with fees and geography. A community that wants sovereignty needed a checkout that matched the values.

## What I built

Started in Svelte on the frontend and Lightning checkout. Later product: what a course buy should feel like when the payment is a satoshi, not a form.

## One hard decision

Treat the payment protocol as UX, not a plugin. If checkout feels like a bank, the product lost.
