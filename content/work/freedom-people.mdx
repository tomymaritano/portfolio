## Problem

Course platforms sit on card processors. Fees, geography, a form. This community wanted checkout that matched how they already pay.

## What I built

Svelte frontend. Lightning checkout. Then the product question: what a course buy should feel like when the payment is a satoshi, not a form.

## One hard decision

Treat the payment protocol as UX, not a plugin. If checkout feels like a bank, the product lost.
