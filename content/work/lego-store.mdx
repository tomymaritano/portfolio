## Problem

A marketplace theme has to look specific and stay generic. LEGO is the demo. Not the product.

## What I built

A Next.js shop meant to sell as a template. Catalog, cart, theme tokens.

## One hard decision

Tokens over hard-coded brand. If you cannot re-skin it in an afternoon, it is not a template.
