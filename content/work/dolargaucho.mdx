## Problem

Macro in Argentina is a feed of numbers, screenshots, and group chats. People who need a weekly read still assemble it by hand.

## What I built

DolarGaucho is a product around that domain: quotes, context, and a surface you can return to. Not a Twitter thread. Not a spreadsheet. A product.

## One hard decision

Treat the number as a UI problem. The quote is not the product — the product is how fast someone can trust the strip and move on.
