## Problem

Argentine macro arrives as numbers, screenshots, and group chats. The weekly read is still assembled by hand.

## What I built

The week's desk — *pulso*. Quotes (official, blue, MEP, CCL), a stress index with a word, signals as sentences, and a short read. RSS and a mail bulletin are the same desk, not a second product. Public sources. Not financial advice.

## One hard decision

The quote is not the product. The product is how fast someone can trust the strip. Treat the number as a UI problem. A series published by someone else stays theirs.
