## Problem

Argentine macro arrives as numbers, screenshots, and group chats. The weekly read is still assembled by hand.

## What I built

Quotes, context, and a model that reads the week. Not a thread. Not a spreadsheet. A product someone can trust and move on from.

## One hard decision

The quote is not the product. The product is how fast someone can trust the strip. Treat the number as a UI problem.
